## Findings
Over time, Netflix's selection of new titles has grown dramatically.Notable rises following 2015 demonstrated how Netflix broadened its selection of content as the site expanded internationally.

The majority of the dataset's titles were published after 2000. Only a handful of older titles—which might be legendary films or television series—are featured. When compared to the majority of the content, these earlier titles can be regarded as anomalies.

Compared to TV shows, movies comprise a greater percentage of Netflix titles. However, TV series continue to make up a sizable portion of the library, demonstrating Netflix's commitment to both forms.
